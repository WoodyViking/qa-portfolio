# 03: NIS2 Directive (cybersecurity)

**Directive (EU) 2022/2555.** Member states had to transpose it by 17 October 2024.
Sweden transposes it through the Cybersecurity Act (*cybersäkerhetslagen*),
which **verify**: entered into force in January 2026.

## What it is

NIS2 requires "essential" and "important" entities in critical sectors to
manage cybersecurity risk, report significant incidents (early warning within
24 h), and hold management accountable.

## Does it apply to hakoo.eu?

**No.** NIS2 uses a **size cap**. It generally applies only to organisations
that are at least **medium-sized** (50+ employees, or more than EUR 10 million in annual
turnover or balance sheet) **and** operate in a sector listed in Annex I or II.

Annex II includes "digital providers" (online marketplaces, search engines and
social networking platforms). A hobby clan site run by one person is far below
the size threshold.

The size-independent exceptions (DNS services, TLD registries, trust services,
public electronic communications networks, and similar) do not describe us.

## Why still read it

NIS2's risk-management list (Art. 21) is a good checklist for any website:

| NIS2 measure | hakoo.eu good practice |
|---|---|
| Risk analysis and security policies | Keep a short list of what could go wrong (leaked `<CONFIG_FILE>`, hijacked Discord bot token) |
| Incident handling | Write down who does what if the site or bot is compromised; see the GDPR breach routine |
| Business continuity, backups | Back up the database and `public_html/` regularly; the repo `backup/` folder can hold the procedure |
| Supply-chain security | Pin third-party libraries (tesseract.js), and only load scripts from `'self'` (the CSP already does this) |
| Secure development | Code review, and never commit secrets |
| Cryptography | HTTPS everywhere, signed cookies (HMAC), strong `<API_SECRET>` |
| Access control and MFA | Enable **2FA** on GitHub, Discord (bot owner account), <HOSTING_PANEL> and email |

## What it means for hakoo.eu

No legal obligations. Adopt the table above as good practice, starting with
2FA on all admin accounts and a tested backup.
