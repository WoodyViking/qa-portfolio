# 08: e-Commerce Directive and consumer law

## e-Commerce Directive

**Directive 2000/31/EC.** Sweden implements it through *lag (2002:562) om
elektronisk handel och andra informationssamhällets tjänster*.

**Art. 5**: providers of information society services must give easy, direct
and permanent access to their name, geographic address, email address and, where
relevant, company registration and VAT number.

**Does it apply?** It covers services *normally provided for remuneration*
(the same test as the DSA, see file 04). A non-commercial clan site is probably
outside it, but a visible **contact email** costs nothing and also covers the
DSA point of contact. We do **not** need to publish a home address as a
private non-commercial operator.

## Consumer law

The following only become relevant if we **sell** something (merchandise, VIP
roles, donations with rewards, paid bot features):

- **Consumer Rights Directive** 2011/83/EU: pre-contract information and a 14-day
  right of withdrawal (special rules for digital content)
- **Unfair Commercial Practices Directive** 2005/29/EC: no misleading offers,
  and clear labelling of advertising and sponsored content
- **Omnibus Directive** (EU) 2019/2161: price-reduction rules and transparency
  about reviews
- **Payment** providers bring their own requirements (PSD2 strong customer
  authentication is handled by the provider)

**Streamer / influencer content**: if clan members promote sponsored products on
the site (for example, via Twitch links), mark it clearly as advertising.
